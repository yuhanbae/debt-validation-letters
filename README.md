# Debt Validation Letters — Free Templates & Guide

If a debt collector is contacting you, the **Fair Debt Collection Practices Act
(FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. § 1692g** gives you the right to demand they prove the debt
before you pay — and to make them stop contacting you entirely
(**§ 1692c(c)**). This repo gives you the two letters that matter most, free.

## Free template 1: Debt Validation Demand

Send within **30 days** of receiving the collector's validation notice, by
certified mail with return receipt.

```text
[Your Name]                                    [Date]
[Your Address]
[City, State ZIP]

[Collector Name]
[Collector Address]

Re: Request for Validation of Debt — Account [Account Number]

To Whom It May Concern:

I dispute the above-referenced debt and request validation under the Fair Debt
Collection Practices Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1692g. I do not acknowledge this debt.
Please provide: (1) verification of the debt, including the name and address of
the original creditor and a copy of the agreement establishing my obligation;
(2) an itemization of principal, interest, fees, and the dates applied; and
(3) documentation of your license to collect in my state and the date this debt
was assigned to you.

Until you provide validation, you must cease all collection efforts on this
account under 15 U.S.C. § 1692g(b). Do not contact me by phone, email, text,
or through any third party.

I am sending this letter by certified mail, return receipt requested.

Sincerely,
[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
```

## Free template 2: Cease and Desist

Stops collector contact (with two narrow statutory exceptions: one confirmation
call, and notice of a specific intended legal action).

```text
[Your Name]                                    [Date]
[Your Address]

[Collector Name]
[Collector Address]

Re: Cease and Desist All Communication — Account [Account Number]

I am exercising my rights under 15 U.S.C. § 1692c(c). Cease all further
communication with me regarding the alleged debt above, except as that section
permits: (1) advice that collection efforts are stopping, or (2) notice of a
specified remedy you intend to invoke. Any communication violating this demand
may constitute an FDCPA violation and will be documented and reported.

Sincerely,
[Your Signature]
[Your Printed Name]
```

## The checklist that makes them stick

1. **Certified mail, return receipt** — under $7 at the post office. The
   receipt's date is what starts every downstream deadline.
2. **Address the exact department** from their validation notice, not "payments".
3. **Photograph all three artifacts**: the receipt, your letter copy, the signed
   green card.
4. **Log every call**: date, time, caller ID. Calls before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m.,
   threats, or telling your employer about the debt are violations worth up to
   **$1,000 statutory damages** each.
5. **Dispute all three bureaus** (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) in the same week
   under FCRA § 1681i — bureaus must investigate within 30 days.
